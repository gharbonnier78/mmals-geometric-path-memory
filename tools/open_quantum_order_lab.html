12-year-old view

Imagine a marble inside a transparent ball. One machine spins the marble's direction. Another machine pulls it toward the top and makes its sideways motion smaller. Spinning first and pulling second does not usually end at the same place as pulling first and spinning second.

Engineer view

The two channels are affine maps on the Bloch vector. Their composition is order-sensitive because the rotation and anisotropic contraction/translation generally do not commute. The trace distance between the two final density matrices is an operationally meaningful order-gap metric.
